Langley & Surrey Market Snapshot

A quick, plain-language read on what's happening across price points right now — update this post regularly to keep it fresh for search and for returning visitors.

Entry-level condos & townhomes

This segment tends to move fastest when rates dip, since it's the most sensitive to monthly payment changes. Watch inventory levels here as a leading indicator for the rest of the market.

Detached homes

Detached inventory in Langley and Surrey varies significantly by sub-area — days-on-market and price-per-square-foot can differ block to block, which is where local knowledge matters most.

What this means if you're selling

Pricing strategy should reflect the specific segment your home competes in, not a city-wide average. A home priced against the wrong comparable set will either sit on market or leave money on the table.

What this means if you're buying

Segments with more inventory give buyers more negotiating room. Knowing which segment you're shopping in — and how it's currently behaving — changes how aggressive an offer needs to be.
