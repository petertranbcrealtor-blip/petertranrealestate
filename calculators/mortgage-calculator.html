How this estimate works

This calculator uses the standard Canadian mortgage payment formula based on your entered rate, amortization period, and payment frequency. It does not include property tax, strata/condo fees, home insurance, or CMHC mortgage default insurance premiums (required on down payments under 20%) — all of which affect your actual carrying cost.
